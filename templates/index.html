01 THE PROJECT

Turning MRI scans
into meaningful segmentation.

Brain Tumor MRI Segmentation is a Medical AI project designed to identify and segment tumor regions from brain MRI scans using deep learning and computer vision.

The system uses a U-Net++ architecture with a ResNet50 encoder to generate precise tumor masks from MRI images.

The project focuses on automated image segmentation and visual analysis while demonstrating the practical application of deep learning in medical imaging.

The model was trained and evaluated on the LGG MRI Segmentation Dataset with dedicated preprocessing and image augmentation techniques.
